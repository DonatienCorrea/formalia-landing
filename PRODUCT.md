## Users
Individuals and households in France (and francophone Europe) who must complete recurring administrative paperwork: passport/CNI renewal, visa/titre de séjour applications, CAF (family/housing benefits) files, housing/rental applications, and insurance enrollment. ## Product Purpose
Formalia is an AI agent that fills out administrative paperwork on the user's behalf. The user answers a short set of questions once (or connects existing documents), and the agent identifies which forms apply, populates them correctly, and prepares them for submission — removing the repetitive, error-prone, and time-consuming manual work of dealing with government and institutional paperwork.

## Positioning
[Assumption] Unlike generic form-filling tools or PDF editors, Formalia acts as an autonomous agent: it determines *which* administrative procedure applies to the user's situation and fills the correct document end-to-end, rather than requiring the user to already know the right form and manually type into it.

## Product Principles
1. Speak to the pain directly: administrative paperwork in France is universally dreaded — lead with relief, not features.
2. Make the mechanism concrete (agent reads your situation → picks the right form → fills it), not abstract "AI-powered" language.
3. Be honest about product maturity: this is an early-access page, so the primary CTA is joining a waitlist / requesting early access, not "buy now".
4. Cover the breadth of supported procedures (passeport, CNI, visas, titre de séjour, CAF, logement, assurances) without implying legal/official endorsement.
